# OdinLink AI engineering principle

One shared AI Brain. Multiple channel adapters. One consistent standard of intelligence, Knowledge grounding, booking correctness, and safety.

For shared conversation defects:
- Trace actual runtime divergence before choosing the correction boundary.
- Fix the shared root cause once; change an adapter only when evidence proves its inputs or handoff are responsible.
- Validate every affected real adapter and the relevant supported languages. Mocked shared-engine entry points alone do not establish channel parity.
- Preserve deterministic booking authority, tenant isolation, document freshness, exact evidence, entailment, authorization, and customer privacy.
- Avoid channel-specific answer overrides, phrase-specific exceptions, and duplicate AI brains.
